# Visual styles

Three styles cover most Arabic social motion work. Pick one per video.

## A. Flat illustrated explainer
Best for: SaaS, POS/apps, restaurants and cafés, services, anything with a "how it works".

- **Look**: flat vector illustration (characters, shop interiors, devices), no outlines or thin ones, soft shadows. Characters in local dress where it fits the audience.
- **Palette**: one dark or warm base + one brand accent + neutrals. Proven combinations:
  - navy `#0E2A5A` + orange `#F28A1E` + white
  - navy `#0B2350` + mint `#DDEFE6` + green `#2E8B3A` + coral `#F28B7C`
  - monochrome warm: cream `#FBE5C4` + brown `#A15E3E` + dark brown `#5A3422`
- **Recurring elements**:
  - a hand holding a phone or POS terminal, screen showing simplified UI; a finger taps a button
  - laptop/tablet/monitor mockups with the product UI
  - capsule "pill" labels with rounded borders for every feature line
  - icon circles (clock, check, location) attached to the side of a pill
  - "?" or "!" thought marks next to a confused character for the problem beat
- **Motion character**: calm. About 40–50% of the runtime is in motion; the rest are short holds of 0.5–2.5s with only secondary motion (rotating rings, steam, blinking).
- **Typical length**: 25–35s vertical.

## B. Kinetic collage (hook reel)
Best for: personal brands, agencies, content creators, opinion/tips reels, awareness.

- **Look**: black-and-white or halftone photo cutouts (people, hands, phones, buildings) with white sticker borders or torn-paper edges, on a plain light or dark background; one neon or saturated accent color for text and highlights (lime `#B6F23A` on charcoal `#262626`, or orange `#F28A1E` on light grey `#E9E8EE`).
- **Text**: very bold display Arabic, large, often tilted or placed along the camera path; one keyword per line in the accent color.
- **Humor beats**: a mascot or meme-like sticker (a smiling vegetable, a cat) popping in for 1–2s to break tension. Use your own mascot, never someone else's.
- **Motion character**: relentless. 70–90% of the runtime is in motion; there are almost no static holds — the camera pans across a large canvas, zooms, or rotates while words land. Readability comes from slow camera speed, not from stopping.
- **Typical length**: 15–35s.
- **Script voice**: conversational dialect, opens with a direct question ("حاير شلون…؟", "زين ليش…؟"), ends with a push ("يلا شمنتظر؟").

## C. Heritage / occasion
Best for: national days, Ramadan/Eid, brand statements, short brand films.

- **Look**: traditional textile/geometric patterns (Sadu, tiles, embroidery), a portrait or scene revealed slowly, rich background.
- **Text**: a short slogan, with **kashida/tatweel** used deliberately to stretch letters as a design element (e.g. "عِـــزّنـــا"), centered, large.
- **Motion character**: build-up then reveal: the title assembles from pattern blocks (pixel/mosaic reveal), the camera slowly pulls back from a detail to the full scene, then a color-tinted overlay settles behind the final title and logo.
- **Typical length**: 8–12s.

## Choosing quickly
| Need | Style |
|---|---|
| explain a product/service step by step | A |
| stop the scroll, opinion, agency self-promo | B |
| occasion greeting, brand statement | C |
| logo reveal | C (pattern) or A (clean), 3–5s |
